Introduction

The Institute Technology Council (ITC) at IIT Bombay is a student-led body focused on promoting technical excellence and innovation across the campus. It serves as a platform to connect students, faculty, and industry, encouraging hands-on projects, research, and skill development. ITC plays a pivotal role in fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and technological exploration among students.

Purpose

The primary purpose of ITC is to: Encourage Innovation: Organize workshops, hackathons, and technical events to develop creative problem-solving skills. Support Student Projects: Provide guidance, mentorship, and resources for technical clubs, competitions, and research initiatives. Facilitate Industry Interaction: Bridge the gap between students and industry experts to expose students to real-world technology and trends. Promote Knowledge Sharing: Conduct seminars, lectures, and conferences to keep the student community updated on emerging technologies.
