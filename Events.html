Past Events

Techfest IIT Bombay:
  • Asia’s largest science and technology festival held every year
  • Includes competitions, exhibitions, lectures, and workshops
  • Attracts participants from across India and globally
Workshops @ Techfest (AI, Robotics, ML, etc.):
  • Hands-on sessions on robotics, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and IoT
  • Conducted with industry experts (e.g., Google, Amazon)
  • Focus on practical learning and real-world applications
TechConnect 2025 IIT Bombay:
  • Exhibition of cutting-edge research and student projects
  • Included symposiums and networking with industry leaders
  • Platform for showcasing innovation and prototypes
ResCon 2025 IIT Bombay:
  • Annual undergraduate research conclave
  • Included keynote talks, competitions, and research presentations
  • Focused on developing research culture among students
